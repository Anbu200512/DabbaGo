The kitchen

Four cooks, one masala, no shortcuts

Ghee, whole spices and a slow tempering — the same base your grandmother would recognise. Nothing is reconstituted and nothing waits in a freezer.

  • Ground spices roasted and ground in-house each week
  • Sesame oil and desi ghee, never vegetable oil
  • No artificial colour, flavour or MSG
The box

Packed so it survives the ride

Steel-grade boxes, a separate curry pot and a sealed roti sleeve. The dal arrives as dal, not as a stain at the bottom of the bag.

  • Every curry sealed in its own leak-proof pot
  • Roti wrapped separately so it never goes soggy
  • Boxes washed and returned with the next drop
The ride

Two windows, one rider, five zones

Lunch goes out between 12:30 and 1:30, dinner between 7:30 and 8:30. Route it once and we keep hitting the same doors.
